Can a small AI read the message on this phone?

This unlisted test loads the same small Qwen model and the same instructions the app would use, then reads twelve fixed synthetic messages on this phone with WebGPU, twice: first with a short prompt, then with the app's full prompt. Six are scams and six are ordinary messages, including warnings that say "never share your code". It does not accept or send your messages.

Pass conditions

  • The model loads on this phone.
  • Each message: 8 seconds or less.
  • Scams: the key request is found in at least 5 of 6.
  • Ordinary messages: a false request is reported in at most 1 of 6.

Before you start

Use Wi-Fi. The model download is about 335 MB the first time. The phone needs at least 1 GB of free storage, or the browser may delete the model and download it again. Keep this tab open and the screen on until it says "Run complete" (24 readings; this can take 5 to 10 minutes).
